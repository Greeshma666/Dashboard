# Source: Various (WTO, FIFA, Visa, research reports)

- FIFA revenue: $11 billion (record-breaking)
- Jobs created: 800,000+

- Less-established teams (Belgium, Austria, Cabo Verde): search interest surged 20x+ during upsets
