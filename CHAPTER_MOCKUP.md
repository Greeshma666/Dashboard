## CHAPTER 5: AND THEN THERE ARE STORIES DATA ALONE CAN'T TELL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

QUESTION: What does it mean for one person?

CAMERA ZOOM: one goalkeeper → one prediction → one match → one mother

---

### OPENING

        And then there are stories
        data alone can't tell.

### THE PREDICTION

        SHE SAID NO ONE WOULD SCORE.

        Before Cabo Verde's first-ever World Cup match,
        Vozinha's mother appeared on Cape Verdean television.

        She predicted no one would score against her son.

### THE MATCH

        Spain took 27 shots.

        Vozinha made 7 saves.

        0 – 0.

        She was right.

### THE ABSENCE

        There was only one person missing.

        His mother couldn't travel.

### THE RESOLUTION

        After Vozinha became emotional discussing her absence,
        the story reached beyond football.

        The U.S. State Department became involved.
        Officials in Praia facilitated her visa.

        She was in the stands for the next match.

### EMOTIONAL PAYOFF

        Vozinha. 40 years old.
        Cabo Verde's first World Cup.
        His mother's prediction. Her presence.

        Some moments don't fit in a dataset.
        They live in the people who were there.

---

### VISUALIZATION TYPE:
- Almost no chart. Pure editorial storytelling.
- "27 shots → 7 saves → 0-0" as minimal visual (large typography only)
- Or: a single restrained graphic showing 27 dots (shots) with 7 highlighted (saves)
- The whole section should feel like a film's quiet final scene
- Enormous whitespace. Let it breathe.

### IMAGE/BACKGROUND:
- Very dark, almost black
- No busy imagery — negative space creates intimacy
- Single warm tone (like candlelight or one stadium light)
- Completely different texture from big-number chapters

### TRANSITION TO CLOSING:

        (generous whitespace)

        The world held its breath.

        9 million gathered without a ticket.
        178 countries shared one city.
        891 players lived their first dream.
        One mother saw her son keep his promise.

        Then it exhaled together.
